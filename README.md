# ShotSathi Live

A pose guide camera that runs in the phone browser. Pick a reference photo or a built-in pose, line it up on the live camera, and Pose Assist tells the subject how to move. Auto-Capture takes the shot once the pose matches.

- Lift a person from any photo (or tap to lift a pet or object)
- Drag, pinch, rotate, mirror and fade the overlay
- Live coaching: step closer/back, move, raise/lower a hand, head and chin
- Auto-Capture, timer, grid, level, flash and zoom where the phone supports them
- English and Bangla
- Runs on the device with MediaPipe; photos never leave the phone

Open the GitHub Pages link on your phone and add it to the home screen.
